4What the Service Is and Is Not+

4.1 What it is

Ocreda is a personal knowledge management application. It stores notes you write, converts them into numerical representations called embeddings so that related material can be located by similarity rather than by keyword, and surfaces older notes it judges relevant to what you are currently writing. It also records which surfaced notes you engage with, and uses those signals to adjust what it surfaces for you in future.

4.2 Surfacing happens without you asking

The defining behavior of the Service is that it shows you your own older notes unprompted, while you write. You are not required to search, tag, or link anything for this to happen. By using the Service you understand and accept that material you wrote in the past, including material you have forgotten writing, may appear on screen without any action by you.

4.3 What it is not

The Service is not, and must not be relied on as: a backup or archival service; a social network; a licensed professional service of any kind, including medical, psychological, legal, tax, or financial advice; a records system for regulated or professional obligations; or a marketplace.
