The short version

Ocreda stores the notes you write and reads them automatically so it can show you older notes while you write. To do that, it sends your note text to two US companies: Supabase, which hosts the database, and Anthropic, which runs the Claude AI model that identifies connections. No human at Ocreda reads your notes as a matter of course.

We do not sell your notes. We do not use them to train AI models. We do not have advertising or data partners.

The beta is open to adults in the United States only, and everything is stored in the United States.

This is unreleased software. Keep your own copies of anything you care about.

You can delete everything at any time by writing to contact@ocreda.com.

The sections below give the full detail. Nothing in this summary overrides them.

2Which Laws Apply, Honestly+

We are a pre-revenue company running a small closed beta. Most United States privacy statutes, including the California Consumer Privacy Act and the Florida Digital Bill of Rights, apply only to businesses above revenue or data-volume thresholds that we do not currently meet. We are not going to claim compliance with a list of statutes we have not been assessed against.

Instead, we have chosen to extend the core rights those laws provide, set out in Section 9, to every beta tester as a matter of contract. Section 15 explains how to complain if we fall short.

Two laws do apply to us regardless of size:

  • Section 5 of the FTC Act, which prohibits deceptive statements about data practices. This notice is written to be accurate rather than reassuring, and where the two conflict we have chosen accurate.
  • Florida’s breach notification statute (Fla. Stat. § 501.171), which governs what we must tell you if data is exposed.

The GDPR and the UK GDPR do not apply, because we do not offer the beta in Europe or the United Kingdom. See Section 5. If you are located there, please do not sign up. We have not put Standard Contractual Clauses or an Article 27 representative in place, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.

4How Your Notes Are Processed+

This is the section that matters most, so it is written plainly.

Your notes are read automatically. When you write a note, its full text is converted into an embedding and stored alongside the original text. When you write, the Service compares what you are writing against your existing notes and sends the most relevant excerpts, together with what you are currently writing, to an AI model so that it can describe how they connect.

This means your note text leaves our database and is transmitted to a third party. Specifically:

Who What they receive What they do with it Where
Supabase, Inc. Your name, email, note text, embeddings, and interaction records Hosts our database, authentication, and storage United States
Anthropic, PBC Excerpts of your notes and the text you are currently writing Runs the Claude API that identifies and explains connections between notes United States

This list is complete as of the Effective Date. If we add or change a subprocessor we will update this notice and email you before the change takes effect.

No human at Ocreda reads your notes in the ordinary course. We may access specific content only if you ask us to while resolving a support issue, or if access is strictly necessary to investigate a security incident or comply with a legal obligation. We keep an internal log of any such access.

Automated processing is still processing. We want to be clear that “no human reads it” is not the same as “we do not process it.” We process the full text of everything you write. That is how the product works.

7What We Will Never Do+

We will never sell your personal data. Not for money, not for other valuable consideration, and not under any definition used in United States state privacy law.

We will never share or license your notes to a commercial partner. We have no such partners. If that were ever to change, we would ask for your affirmative opt-in consent first. We would not do it through a policy update.

We will never use your notes to train AI models. Not our own, and not anyone else’s. Our arrangements with our AI provider are on terms under which submitted content is not used for model training.

We do not show your notes to other users. Ocreda is single-user by design. Nothing you write is visible to another tester, and your data does not influence what any other tester sees.

What we do instead. We personalize retrieval within your own Account, using your interaction signals to rank your own notes for you. That is different from model training, and we describe it as what it is.

8Legal Basis and Consent+

We process your data on two grounds:

  • To perform our agreement with you. Storing and retrieving your notes is the service you asked for.
  • With your consent. Your consent covers processing your note content through the AI subprocessor described in Section 4, which is presented to you at sign-up rather than buried here.

You may withdraw consent at any time by closing your Account. Because the AI processing in Section 4 is how the product functions, withdrawing consent means the Service can no longer be provided to you, and your data will be deleted under Section 11.

10Security+

We protect your data with:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Row-level security policies in our database, so that queries cannot return another tester’s rows
  • Password hashing, so we never store your password
  • Access controls limiting internal access to the minimum necessary, with access logged
  • Encrypted backups
  • Incident response procedures

What we are not claiming. We have not completed a third-party security audit, we do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification, and we have not commissioned a penetration test. We are a small pre-release product and we would rather tell you that than imply a security posture we do not have. Section 3.3 of the Terms of Service reflects this.

If something goes wrong. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you by email without unreasonable delay and no later than thirty (30) days after we confirm it, and we will notify the Florida Attorney General where the law requires. Our notice will tell you what was exposed, when, what we have done, and what you should do.
