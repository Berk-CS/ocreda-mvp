Action Over Overthinking

If you feel that you are overthink about something and delaying action, ask yourself.

How can I ensure that I become worse at what I am trying to accomplish?

Then flip it and you will see what you must do to become better.

E.g. "I want to become a better writer”
Do not write
Write inconsistently
Write about things you find boring

Flip them around:
Write
Write consistently
Write about things that excite you
