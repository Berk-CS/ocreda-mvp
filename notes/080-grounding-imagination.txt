Grounding Imagination

The person who lives in a world of his own in his head, loses touch with reality and what is trying to tell him.

Imagination if not controlled can be very dangerous.

Can rob you the ability to think that he even needed to act.

We start to believe what we tell ourselves, good and bad things, which they are never a 100% true. We must live in the tangible and real.

Forget the world we created for ourselves, the stories, the fake feedback and live the now, act on it and create it with actual experiences.
