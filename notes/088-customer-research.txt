Customer Research

1. Understanding customers (qualitative)
User interviews / surveys: Typeform, Google Forms, SurveyMonkey
Behavioral insight: Hotjar, FullStory (session recordings, heatmaps)

2. Sizing the market (quantitative)
Industry data: Statista, IBISWorld, Gartner, CB Insights
Search demand: Google Trends, Ahrefs, SEMrush (keyword volume = proxy for interest)

3. Competitive analysis
SimilarWeb: traffic and engagement estimates for competitors
App Annie/data.ai: app store rankings, downloads
