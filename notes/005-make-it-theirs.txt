Make It Theirs

People don't like to be told what to do or be sold something.

They like to be consulted, they like the importance of giving unique advice.

So plant they idea in their heads, make them think that they came up with that idea, thank them, and give them the credit of that idea. They will feel like they owe you something.

You can start this process by making sudden suggestions or asking them as if they could give you some advice on the topic you want them to develop.
