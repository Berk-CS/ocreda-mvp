Non-Intrusive Marketing

Marketing shouldn't be intrusive, aim for trust among tribes of people.

They have to ask themselves, "If I buy this, what does it make me?" It should be like a conversation, more of an invitation to belong to the tribe. So you must be clear with what you say, because people only catch one element from the message. Branding: Customers understand who you are and what you do, without you bombarding them with ads.
