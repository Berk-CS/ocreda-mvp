Fear Points to Growth

Fear of your work as well as self doubt can be an ally because it tells you that what you are doing matters.

The more scared we are to do something that is what we should be doing first.

If what we are doing means nothing to us then there will be no resistance.

Great people do things that make them suffer, stretch, actually hard things that they haven't done before. And they avoid always doing for the things that don't challenge them.

So if you are paralyzed with fear is a good sign. It shows you what you have to do.

Explanation: If you are scared it matters.
